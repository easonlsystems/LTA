## Validation and decision

`npm ci` and full `npm test` passed on the new audit worktree; test suite includes build, media fixtures, source matrix, billing, quota, MCP, OAuth, security and runtime smoke. These are local checks, not production certification. No code change was justified by the evidence gathered, and no PR was opened.

**Is current LTA production actually reliable enough for normal users? NO.** A Reel read has failed in current production, extraction/ASR and region backups are absent, top-ups cannot be purchased despite the product claim, and most sources lack fresh live verification. This does not mean every source fails.
